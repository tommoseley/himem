- **Voice — a locked decision applied to one of two surfaces.** `SpeechService` hardcoded `mode: .measurement`, forbidden since 2026-07-15 because it suppresses input GAIN (watch mic ~-40 dBFS, silent clips). Every phone recording has been quieter than it should be; plausibly contributes to the observed transcription coverage shortfall (15.84s of a 21.60s clip). **This is a Judi-on-the-trip fix, not an iPad fix.**

- **Camera/video — a portrait clamp that cannot work on iPad.** `OrientationLock` + `requestGeometryUpdate(.portrait)`; the app declares all four orientations for iPad, so it is multitasking-eligible and iPadOS does not honour a runtime clamp. The picker adapted to an orientation its capture connection did not match — producing exactly the broken preview the clamp exists to prevent, as its own comment describes. iPhone unaffected (portrait-dominant plist).
  - Clamp skipped on iPad. **NOT** narrowing `UISupportedInterfaceOrientations_iPad` — that would "fix" it by breaking multitasking.
