## THE HEADLINE — four wrong theories about one clip, ended by a two-tap fixture check

Cluster A drew 2 of 3 for three sessions. The missing member was the ref-backed clip, so every theory pointed at premise 2 in the cluster path. **All four were wrong.**

**Actual cause:** QA fixture ids are deterministic by design, and `clear` removed rows, refs and files but **not the review mark**. Open a seeded clip once and every re-seed produces its replacement **born reviewed** — present on **All**, absent from **New**, because All applies no review filter.

**It surfaced on the ref-backed clip alone**, and that asymmetry is why it took so long: only its review state lives in the ref-keyed store under a stable id; its manifest-backed siblings are recreated each run with `reviewed: false` and could never have shown the fault. **The one misbehaving clip's one distinguishing property pointed straight at the wrong subsystem — a coincidence of storage read as a causal signal.**

`fb67c4b` is what made it visible: before review stuck for refs, the mark did not survive to bite.
