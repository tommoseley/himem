HiMem · watchOS · Lo-fi

A glanceable mic.

Two features for v1: capture a voice contribution (online or off, the watch always stores locally first), and see/manage what's pending sync. Three ways in — complication, app icon, Siri. The canonical recording UI is a big centered timer, a live waveform, and a Stop & save / Next pair at the bottom — with a corner ✕ for Cancel. Black background, ochre accent, no chrome the wrist doesn't earn. Drawn for the 49mm Ultra; designs flex down to 45mm without rework. See Watch · spec.md for locked rules.

1 · Entry points

Three ways the user starts a recording. The complication is the fastest path — one tap from the watch face. The app icon is the obvious fallback. Siri is the hands-free path; we treat it as a third option, not a primary surface.

1b · Fresh-start countdown · 3 · 2 · 1 · Listening

A 3-second gather-yourself pause after the user taps to start. Borrowed wholesale from Apple Workout: bright ochre ring draws in clockwise from 180° while "Ready" sits centered, then a dim ochre overlay overwrites it counter-clockwise from 180° during the 3-second countdown. Continuous sweep. Soft haptic per second. No loud counting, no ticking, no "Listening" beat — the ring closing at the top is the "go." Tap anywhere to cancel instantly. Fresh starts only. The Next button on a roll bypasses the countdown — the whole point of Next is the mic never pauses, and a 3-second wait would kill that contract.

Countdown rules

  • Two-phase ring, Workout-style. Phase 1 (~600ms): bright ochre ring draws in clockwise from 180° while "Ready" is centered. Phase 2 (~3000ms): dim ochre overwrites the bright ring counter-clockwise from 180°, eating it down to a crescent at 12 o'clock. Continuous sweep, not steps. When the crescent closes at 0°, recording begins.
  • No "Listening" beat. The ring closing at the top is the "go." Don't double the signal.
  • Numerals are passengers. 3 → 2 → 1 swap in time with the second-ticks, but the ring's continuous sweep is the primary signal. The numbers help the user count down; the ring is what the eye actually tracks.
  • Fresh-start only. Tapping the mic complication / app icon / Siri opens directly into the countdown. The on-a-roll Next button bypasses it — the mic-never-pauses contract holds.
  • Soft haptic per second. .click on watch, UISelectionFeedbackGenerator.selectionChanged on phone. Three pulses total. Not a buzz, not ticking audio.
  • Tap anywhere to cancel. No ✕ press required — the whole screen is the cancel target. Returns to the previous surface (Capture page on watch, Today on phone, parent Memory on append).
  • Color palette. Bright = #E55A22 (a brightened ochre, more saturated than the brand --accent for countdown readability on cream). Dim = #9A3815. Clear contrast between the two so the sweep edge reads.
  • Settings opt-out. Power users can disable in Settings → Capture → Skip countdown. Default on. Reduced Motion users see the numbers without ring animation but countdown still runs.

Recording rules

  • Stop & save is the only intentional commit path. Cream hero pill, bottom-left.
  • Next is the on-a-roll affordance. Ochre glyph button, bottom-right of Stop. See §2b.
  • Cancel is the top-left ✕ corner glyph — demoted from the commit row. Two-tap confirm before discard.
  • Palm-cover dims the watch but does not discard. Earlier “palm = cancel” behavior is retired (destructive + undiscoverable).
  • Wrist-off auto-stops and saves. We never discard a recording the user walked away from.
  • Live waveform is the “audio is rolling” contract. Never resets across Next; never pauses mid-record.
  • Hard cap at 5 minutes per clip. Warning at 4:45 (amber + “30s left”); auto-stop at 5:00.
  • Local storage cap: 50 unsynced clips. Next dims at 49/50; recording blocked at 50.
  • Counter is the signal capture is alive. Drive from Date() since clip start, not recorder currentTime.
2b · Next clip · keep the roll

One button: end this clip, start a new one. No screen change. No confirm. The user is on a roll — we don't make them stop, navigate back, re-tap to record. Each tap of Next commits the current clip, resets the counter to 0:00, and increments the persistent Clip N · on a roll line under the timer. Recording continues uninterrupted; the waveform never resets. Clips group into one Memory on phone via existing session rules + rollGroupId.

Edge state

At 49/50 unsynced, the Next button dims to 45% opacity and a small Sync soon label appears under it. Tap is ignored. The current clip can still finish via Stop & save — commit is never blocked. At 50/50, recording is blocked altogether (screen-level warning).

On-a-roll rules

  • Each tap of Next = one new Clip — not a new Memory. Clips group into one Memory on phone via session rules + rollGroupId.
  • Persistent state line (Clip N · on a roll) replaces the earlier 1.5s flash eyebrow. A user mid-thought won't catch a transient.
  • Min clip length 2s. Double-tap below the threshold is silently debounced — no haptic, no save, no reset. Protects against rolling-thumb taps.
  • 5-min cap is per clip, not per roll. Splitting works with the cap, not around it.
  • Cancel during a roll discards only the current unfinished clip. Earlier Next-clips are already committed. Copy: “Discard this clip? Earlier clips in this recording are already saved.”
  • Wrist-off auto-stops the current clip and saves it; earlier clips in the roll were committed at each Next tap, so nothing is at risk.
  • Storage near full (49/50 unsynced): Next dims with Sync soon label. Stop & save still works.
  • Phone equivalent: same gesture, different model — on phone, Next creates a new audio segment within one direct-to-memory recording. See On a roll · spec.md.

Sync confidence rules

  • Online success: 2s toast, auto-dismiss.
  • Offline save: explicit toast, stays until wrist drops or tap.

Tap behavior — the real design question.

From the complication, when I tap…

Open HiMem ready to record One tap to record — explicit Stop & save commits. Safer; one extra step.
Press & hold to record immediately Walkie-Talkie style. Faster; risks accidental recordings on wrist bumps.
Default A. Power users opt into B once they trust the gesture. Setting lives in iPhone → HiMem → Watch.

Complication rules

  • Glyph & ochre only. No live transcription, battery state, duration on face.
  • Pending count appears only when > 0. Calm by default.
  • Recording state is rare — complication only shows it if HiMem is actively recording in the background.
  • Sync issue dot only after 24h of failure. Don't cry wolf.
  • Tap routing respects the user's setting (default: open to record-ready, not auto-record).

Decisions to validate

Settled

  • Recording surface: V1 (centered mic + counter + Stop & save, with demoted Cancel link).
  • Wrist-off stops and saves automatically. Never discards.
  • Local storage cap: 50 unsynced recordings.
  • Watch is a capture queue, not a viewer — pending list only, no Memory Box browsing.

Open questions

  • App closed mid-recording — watchOS backgrounds drop the mic. Reach for the background-task API, or surface as “keep watch on wrist while recording.”
  • Siri (App Intent) — ship in v1 alongside complication, or follow-up release?
