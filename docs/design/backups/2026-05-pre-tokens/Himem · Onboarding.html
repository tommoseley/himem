Notes

Decisions worth remembering when you build this.

Why no separate sign-in screen

Sign in with Apple is biometric. One tap → Face ID → account exists. There's nothing to type, nothing to verify, nothing to recover. A separate sign-in screen would be performative.

App-lock (Face ID on every cold launch) is a different feature — ships as a Settings toggle in v1.1, not in onboarding.

Permission framing

  • Mic: required, explained as enabling the product, not asking for trust.
  • Notifications: framed by what they earn ("a small nudge when an inference needs you"), not "stay engaged".
  • Photos: optional, deferred when first used if skipped.

iOS shows the system prompt only after the user toggles. We never lie about what we'll do with the access.

No paywall in onboarding

Free trial is silent. The user gets the full app from day one. The paywall surfaces after their 7th capture or day 14, whichever comes first — they hit it having already gotten value.

Hard-paywalling first-run punishes the curious and is the #1 reason apps die in TestFlight.

What we cut

  • A "what is HiMem" carousel. Three swipeable promo screens are a tax. The product itself teaches better.
  • iCloud opt-in screen. Sign in with Apple implies the iCloud account; CloudKit is automatic.
  • Avatar / display-name setup. Apple gives us a name. We use it. Edit later in Settings.

Hand-off, not handhold

The last screen is empty Today with a written prompt. We don't simulate a fake capture. We don't run a tutorial overlay. We get out of the way.

Skip is honored

Skip on Permissions or Topics doesn't trap the user. They land in the same Today with the same prompt. Topics shows a "Set up topics" gentle banner the first 3 sessions, then disappears.

Re-onboarding

If a user signs in on a new device, they see only screen 1 (welcome → continue) and land directly on Today with their existing bin synced. No permissions or topics — those are restored from CloudKit.

Voice + tone

Sentences read as if spoken by a thoughtful friend, not an app. "Half-formed thoughts" not "your second brain." Italic serif for warmth (1 line max per screen); SF for everything functional.
